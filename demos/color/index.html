Demos / Wide-gamut color

Reds that don't exist in sRGB.

Every iPad sold for the last several years ships with a Display P3 panel. CSS lets you address it directly — the brightest reds, greens, and blues in P3 are visibly more saturated than the brightest reds, greens, and blues in sRGB.

Your display is reporting … color gamut. (See: @media (color-gamut: p3))

Caveats

The P3 swatches will look identical to the sRGB swatches if you're viewing this on an sRGB display — the browser clamps. On an iPad, there's a visible jump from one to the next.

color-mix() is independent of gamut — it just blends two colors in a chosen interpolation space. Mixing in oklab tends to keep perceived lightness even.
